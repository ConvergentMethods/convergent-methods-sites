SMS Privacy Policy

Scope

This policy covers the Convergent Methods LLC Atlas SMS notification program. The program is limited to private operational notifications to the designated account owner.

Information and use

We process the destination phone number, consent and opt-out records, message content, timestamps, replies, and delivery information to send requested notifications, handle responses, honor opt-outs, and diagnose delivery problems.

Service providers

Twilio and telecommunications carriers process information needed to transmit messages. Website hosting providers may process ordinary request information when these pages are viewed. Access to internal messaging records is restricted to authorized operators and systems.

No marketing sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Necessary messaging service providers receive information only to operate the service.

Retention and choices

We retain records needed to operate and troubleshoot the service, document consent, and honor opt-outs. Reply STOP to stop notifications. To request access, correction, or deletion of messaging information, contact william.everett.wright@gmail.com. Some records may be retained to honor suppression requests or meet applicable obligations.
